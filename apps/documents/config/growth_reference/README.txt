Growth reference tables (age_months, L, M, S).
*_who.csv    WHO Child Growth Standards 2006, birth to 60 months.
*_iap.csv    Used for children over 60 months. Today these files hold the WHO Growth Reference 2007 (5-19 years): height-for-age to 228 months, weight-for-age to 120 months only.
             Source: https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years  (expanded z-score tables, de Onis et al., Bull WHO 2007;85:660-7).
             older_children_source.txt names what these files really are, so the chart says "WHO 2007". Replace the four *_iap.csv with the IAP 2015 LMS tables and delete older_children_source.txt to show "IAP 2015" instead.
